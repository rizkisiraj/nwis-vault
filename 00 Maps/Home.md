# NWIS / TGMDP Learning Vault

Learning notes on system design and architecture, written from a walk through the NWIS_Legacy workspace (`~/Documents/NWIS_Legacy`).

## How to use this vault
- New here? Read [[How to Navigate This Vault]] first (2 minutes), then [[Learning Path]].
- Begin with [[System Overview]]. It shows how the pieces fit together.
- Next, read one codebase map and follow its links into concepts and patterns.
- Open the **Graph view** (Cmd+G) to see how the notes connect. Colours show folders: codebases, concepts, patterns, practices and legacy.

## Maps
- [[System Overview]]: the whole system on one page
- [[Codebases MOC]]: every repo and its architecture
- [[Concepts MOC]]: foundational ideas (HTTP, DI, transactions, state…)
- [[Design Patterns MOC]]: named patterns and where they appear
- [[Best Practices MOC]]: conventions worth copying
- [[Legacy to Modern MOC]]: what the rewrite changed and why
- [[Learning Path]]: a suggested reading order
- [[How to Navigate This Vault]]: structure, reading modes and graph tips
